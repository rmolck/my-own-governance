# Decisions

Record only consequential decisions. Use the following structure and remove this instruction once adopted.

## D-NNN — Concise decision title

**Status/date:** Record the durable status and ISO date when useful.

**Context:** State verified forces and constraints; distinguish evidence from assumptions.

**Decision:** State the material choice and its authority.

**Rationale:** Explain why this alternative was selected.

**Consequences:** Record trade-offs, follow-up obligations, and conditions for reconsideration.
